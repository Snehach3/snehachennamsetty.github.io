Dr Sneha Chennamsetty

Researcher and Faculty, Department of Space Studies
Management Educational Research Institute, Delhi

I work on wireless communication systems, from 4G and 5G to 6G, with a focus on deep learning for signal processing and intelligent communication. I use software-defined radios (SDRs) for real-time experiments, system design, and rapid prototyping.

I completed my PhD in Communication Systems at Mahindra University, Hyderabad, in 2026. My thesis studied deep learning-based analytical and experimental approaches to OFDM and OTFS systems.

Research interests

  • 5G and 6G wireless systems
  • Deep learning for OFDM and OTFS
  • PAPR reduction techniques
  • Software-defined radio testbeds and spectrum measurement
  • Signal and image processing, including remote sensing and biomedical imaging
