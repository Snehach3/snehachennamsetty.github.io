Doctoral research

Deep Learning-Based Analytical and Experimental Investigations of OFDM and OTFS Wireless Communication Systems

PhD, Mahindra University, Hyderabad. 2021 to 2026

My thesis combines theory, deep learning and hardware experiments to study OFDM and OTFS, two modulation schemes central to 4G, 5G and emerging 6G systems. The work covers:

  • Neural network models: a unified neural network model for OFDM and OTFS modulation, and neural network based OTFS systems.
  • PAPR reduction: the classical selected mapping technique applied to OTFS and OTSM modulation.
  • Testbeds: analysis and emulation of testbeds for OFDMA 4G and 5G cellular networks, and real-time measurement of a secured wireless communication testbed.
  • Spectrum measurement: real-time 4G and 5G spectrum occupancy measurements in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

Details are in the publications list.

Projects

  • Image processing on prostate cancer (DST project)

    GVP College of Engineering, Visakhapatnam. September to November 2019. MATLAB

    Preprocessed and analyzed prostate MRI images to improve visualization and extract features that indicate cancerous tissue. Used noise reduction, segmentation and morphological operations to isolate the region of interest, then extracted statistical and texture features to support diagnosis and possible classification.

  • Image retrieval of biomedical images (DST project)

    IIIT Sri City, Chittoor. January to September 2019. MATLAB

    Developed a content-based image retrieval system that finds similar MRI scans in a medical image database. Extracted texture and shape features, implemented preprocessing, feature extraction and similarity measurement, and evaluated performance with precision-recall metrics.

  • Super-resolution of satellite images (M.Tech project)

    National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC), Hyderabad. MATLAB

    Remote sensing images lose detail because of the atmosphere, sensors and other conditions. This project used super-resolution to reconstruct high-resolution images from low-resolution ones, to support more detailed analysis.

  • Low-transition test pattern generator (B.Tech project)

    Bhimavaram Institute of Engineering and Technology. Xilinx

    Used an LT-LFSR to reduce average and peak power during circuit testing by cutting transitions between consecutive test patterns and between bits. The generator works with both BIST and scan-based BIST architectures.
